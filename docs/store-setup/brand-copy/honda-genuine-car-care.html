Honda Genuine Car Care is a range of premium vehicle protection products developed exclusively for Honda vehicles by Protection Brands and approved by Honda Australia.

Genuine Protection for Every Journey

Designed specifically for Honda owners, the range provides comprehensive protection inside and out. Advanced Graphene Ceramic Paint Protection helps maintain gloss and defend against UV exposure, environmental contaminants and everyday wear, while Interior Protection treatments help protect leather, fabric, vinyl and trim from spills, stains and soiling.

The range also includes Window Tinting, Antimicrobial Treatments and Genuine Honda maintenance products, providing a complete vehicle care solution.
