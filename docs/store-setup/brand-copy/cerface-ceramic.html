Protection Beyond the Surface.

Advanced Graphene Ceramic Protection. Exclusively Available Through Premium Automotive Dealerships.

Developed by Protection Brands, CERFACE is a premium vehicle protection system designed to enhance, protect and preserve your vehicle’s appearance for years to come. Available through a select network of automotive dealerships, CERFACE combines advanced protection technology with professional application to deliver lasting results and exceptional visual appeal.

At the heart of CERFACE is an advanced Graphene-Enhanced Ceramic Coating that forms a durable protective layer over your vehicle's paintwork. Designed to enhance gloss, colour depth and clarity, it also helps defend against Australia's harsh environmental conditions and everyday contaminants.

Its smooth, hydrophobic finish helps repel water, dirt and road grime, making cleaning easier while helping to maintain a cleaner, more polished appearance. CERFACE also provides resistance to common environmental contaminants such as bird and bat droppings, tree sap, UV exposure and airborne pollutants.

Protection Beyond the Exterior

CERFACE BEYOND Interior Protection complements the exterior coating by helping protect leather, fabric, carpet, vinyl and interior trim surfaces. The invisible protective barrier helps reduce the impact of everyday spills, stains, soiling and UV-related wear, helping maintain the appearance and comfort of your vehicle's interior.
